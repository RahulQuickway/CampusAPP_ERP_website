Home · Features

Features built for campus leadership

Outcome-focused capabilities that reduce paperwork, improve visibility, and keep every stakeholder aligned.

📋

Admissions that convert enquiries into enrolled students

Track every enquiry with structured follow-ups, digital applications, and status visibility so admission teams spend less time chasing forms and more time counselling families.

  • Enquiry capture with source and counsellor assignment
  • Application forms, document checklists, and merit shortlists
  • Seat allocation workflows with clear pipeline stages
🎓

Student information as a single system of record

Maintain accurate profiles, guardians, documents, and academic history — so office, teachers, and parents stop working from conflicting registers.

  • Complete student & guardian profiles
  • Document vault and promotion history
  • Class, section, and house organisation
✅

Attendance that reclaims teaching and office time

Mark daily or period-wise attendance quickly, notify parents when needed, and generate compliance reports without end-of-month spreadsheet panic.

  • Class and period attendance modes
  • Absence alerts and summary reports
  • AI-assisted pattern highlights (product vision)
💳

Fees & billing with transparent collections

Define fee structures, issue receipts, track dues, and manage scholarships — giving finance teams and parents a shared view of what is owed and paid.

  • Flexible fee heads and instalment plans
  • Receipts, dues, and concession handling
  • Reminder suggestions to improve collections
📝

Exams, timetable & academic operations

Plan assessments, capture marks, publish results, and manage class schedules so academic coordinators run smoother exam cycles.

  • Exam scheduling and gradebooks
  • Result publishing and report cards
  • Timetable with faculty and room allocation
📢

Communication that reaches the right people

Circulate notices and circulars to parents, staff, and students with clear targeting — reducing “we didn’t get the message” gaps.

  • Audience-targeted announcements
  • Parent portal visibility for fees & attendance
  • Mobile-ready access for on-the-go teams

Academic year cycles, INR fee patterns, and multi-role campus operations.

Mobile-responsive
